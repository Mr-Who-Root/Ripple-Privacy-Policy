1Overview

In short: Ripple keeps your habits on your phone. There's no account or sign-in, no Ripple server and no analytics SDK. The developer never receives your habits or history. Ripple Free shows ads from Google AdMob; Ripple Pro has no ads.

This policy covers both Android editions of Ripple - Habit Tracker, developed by Govind S R:

  • Ripple Free (com.ripple.app.ripple.free): free, supported by ads.
  • Ripple Pro (com.ripple.app.ripple.pro): paid, with everything unlocked and no ads.

Where the editions differ, this policy says so. Everything else applies to both.

2Information we collect

The developer collects no personal information. Ripple has no account, no sign-in and no server, so there's nowhere for your data to be sent to us.

Data the app keeps on your phone

To work, Ripple saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Your habits (names, icons, colours, card styles, goals, rest days, habit stacks) and every check-in.
  • Reminder times and the completion times that Smart Reminders learn from.
  • Mood journal entries and notes, focus-timer sessions, achievements and challenge progress.
  • Accountability buddies you've added (the display name, emoji and progress in the codes you scanned or pasted) and your own buddy name.
  • Focus Lock settings (each habit's lock times, days and chosen apps), timed app locks and daily app limits.
  • Settings such as theme, accent colour, font, week start and app icon.
  • Ripple Free only: the extra habits, starred habits, card styles, fonts and accent colours you've unlocked by watching rewarded ads.

Screen time (optional)

If you open Screen Time or use Focus Lock, Ripple asks you to turn on Usage access in Android Settings. With it, Ripple reads from Android which apps you used, when and for how long, how often you opened them and how often you unlocked your phone. It uses this only to show your screen time, to enforce the locks and limits you set, and to list your most-used apps first when you choose apps to lock. Ripple reads these numbers when it needs them and doesn't store them, include them in backups or send them anywhere.

To let you choose apps to lock, Ripple reads the list of apps that have a home-screen icon (their names and icons) on your phone. The apps you pick, your lock times and your daily limits stay on your phone; Focus Lock settings and daily limits are included in your backups.

Feedback emails

If you tap Request a Feature or Report a Bug, your own email app opens a draft to the developer with the app version and device model filled in (you can remove them). If you send it, we receive your message and email address and use them only to reply to you and improve Ripple.

3Data storage & security

  • On your device: all app data lives in Ripple's private app storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. Ripple's home-screen widgets read the same on-device data.
  • No cloud of ours: Ripple has no servers and doesn't upload your habits anywhere.
  • Backups you export: Export Database creates a backup file only when you ask, and saves or sends it where you choose. The file isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. Import reads only the file you pick.
  • Android device backup: Ripple doesn't opt out of Android's own backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Ripple's app data in your device backup in your Google account, so it can come back on a new phone. You and Google control this; the developer can't see it.
  • Retention: data stays on your phone until you delete it (see Your choices). Data that Google collects for ads (Ripple Free) is kept under Google's own policies.

4Advertising & analytics

Ripple Free Ads by Google AdMob

  • SDKs: Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent.
  • Ad types: banner ads, native ads between your habits and on some screens, and optional rewarded video ads.
  • Rewarded ads play only when you choose Watch ad to unlock something: one more habit or starred habit, an animated card style for one habit, a font, an accent colour, or a GIF/video export.
  • What Google may collect to show, personalise (where allowed) and measure ads and to prevent fraud: your advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information (model, Android version, language, app version), how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data from the ads SDK.
  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states whose laws require it, Ripple shows Google's consent message before ads are personalised. Change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.

Ripple Pro No ads

  • No advertising SDK and no ads of any kind.
  • No advertising-ID permission.
  • Everything is unlocked, so there are no rewarded ads.

Your habit data is never shared with advertisers. Your habits, check-ins, history, journal, buddies and other app data aren't given to Google, ad networks or advertisers. The developer doesn't receive the ad data above, only aggregated earnings and ad-performance reports from AdMob.

Analytics

Neither edition includes an analytics or crash-reporting SDK. There's no Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics or Performance), no Google Analytics, and no other analytics or tracking library. The only Google services involved are listed under Data sharing; in Ripple Free, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects ad measurement data as described above.

6Data sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your data. Data leaves your phone only in these cases:

  • Things you choose to share: share cards, GIFs, videos and your Wrapped recap are made on your phone and go only where you send them. Backups go only where you save or send them.
  • Accountability buddies: a buddy code holds your display name, avatar, overall streak and progress, and the names of only the habits you choose to share. It passes directly between people by QR code or link, with no server. Codes aren't encrypted, so share them only with people you trust.
  • Google Fonts: app fonts other than System Default (including the default font) are downloaded from Google's servers the first time they're used. Google receives that request, including your IP address. No habit data is included. Choose System Default in Settings › Customization to use only your phone's fonts.
  • Google ML Kit (QR scanning): runs on your phone and doesn't send camera images to Google. It may send Google limited diagnostic and usage information (such as device type, app version and performance) and may download model updates.
  • Google AdMob (Ripple Free only): as described in Advertising & analytics.
  • Legal reasons: since the developer holds no user data, there's nothing to disclose beyond feedback emails you've sent, and only if required by law.

7Your choices & deleting data

  • Delete everything: Settings › Reset all data, or uninstall Ripple. (In Ripple Free, ad unlocks you've earned are kept on reset, since you earned them.)
  • Ads personalisation (Free): change consent in Settings › Ad privacy choices where offered, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads on some phones).
  • Permissions: turn off notifications, camera, usage access or display over other apps in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Focus Lock and limits: end a timed lock in Explore › Screen Time, remove a daily limit from an app's page there, or turn off Focus Lock in the habit's editor.
  • Fonts: pick System Default to stop font downloads.
  • Feedback emails: ask us to delete your emails by writing to the address below.

8Children's privacy

Ripple isn't directed at children under 13, and the developer doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. Ripple Free shows ads from Google AdMob as described above. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we'll delete it.
